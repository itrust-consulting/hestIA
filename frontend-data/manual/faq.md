# FAQ

<details>
<summary>I cannot log in — it says my credentials are incorrect.</summary>

Check that Caps Lock is off and that you are using your username (not your e-mail address, unless your administrator has configured e-mail login). If your organisation uses SSO, use the SSO button rather than the username/password form.

If the problem persists, your account may be temporarily locked out after too many failed attempts, or it may have expired. Contact your **system administrator** to verify your account status.
</details>

<details>
<summary>I am asked to change my password immediately after logging in.</summary>

Your administrator has flagged your account as requiring a password reset — this is common for new accounts or after a manual password reset. Enter your current (temporary) password, choose a new one, and save. You will then be taken to the main interface.
</details>

<details>
<summary>My answer has no citations — is that normal?</summary>

Yes. If your question is general-purpose (writing help, explanations, brainstorming), hestIA answers from its trained knowledge and does not include citations. Citations only appear when the answer is grounded in your organisation's indexed document collections.

If you expected citations but did not get them, try rephrasing the question to be more specific, or confirm with your administrator that the relevant collection is indexed and that you have access to it.
</details>

<details>
<summary>A document I know exists is not being cited.</summary>

Several things could cause this:

1. **Not indexed yet** — the document may not have been uploaded to a collection by an administrator.
2. **Access** — you may not be a member of the tenant that has access to that collection. Contact your tenant moderator.
3. **Classification** — the document's classification level may exceed yours. Contact your administrator.
4. **Language mismatch** — if the document is in a different language to your question, retrieval quality drops significantly. Ask in the same language as the document.
5. **Phrasing** — try rephrasing your question using terms that appear in the document.
</details>

<details>
<summary>The cited passage seems outdated or incorrect.</summary>

hestIA indexes documents at upload time. If the source document has been updated since it was last uploaded, the index reflects the old version. Ask your administrator to re-upload the updated file.
</details>

<details>
<summary>The answer cites a passage but I cannot access the full document.</summary>

hestIA shows you the passage it retrieved, but it does not provide a download link to the original file. If you need the full document, contact your administrator or tenant moderator — they can direct you to where the document is stored.
</details>

<details>
<summary>I attached a file but hestIA does not seem to use it.</summary>

Make sure the file is in a supported format (PDF, DOCX, XLSX/XLSM, PPTX, TXT, CSV, JSON, Markdown) and that it uploaded successfully (you should see it appear in the input bar before sending). If the file is very large or heavily image-based (e.g. a scanned PDF with no text layer), extraction may be limited. Try asking explicitly: "Based on the attached document, …"
</details>

<details>
<summary>I get better results in one language than another.</summary>

Retrieval works best when your question is in the same language as the indexed documents. If your organisation's collections are primarily in French, ask in French. If you need to ask in a different language, be aware that retrieval accuracy may be lower.
</details>

<details>
<summary>My session expired — did I lose my conversations?</summary>

No. Conversations are saved server-side. After logging back in, all your previous conversations will appear in the left sidebar exactly as you left them.
</details>

<details>

</details>
