Operational Notes - Weekly Maintenance Window
==============================================

Maintenance windows occur every Sunday between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC. During this window, non-critical services may be restarted for patching. Critical services (authentication, document ingestion queue) are patched in a rolling fashion to avoid downtime.

Escalation contact: on-call rotation via the incident management system. Do not page the security team for routine maintenance.
