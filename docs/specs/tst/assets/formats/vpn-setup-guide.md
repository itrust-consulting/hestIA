# VPN Setup Guide

## Overview

This guide describes how remote employees connect to internal systems over the organizational VPN.

## Steps

1. Install the approved VPN client on your managed device.
2. Authenticate using your corporate credentials and a second factor.
3. Once connected, internal-only services become reachable; no further application-level configuration is required.

## Troubleshooting

If the connection fails, verify that your device's clock is synchronized, since the VPN gateway rejects handshakes with a significant time skew.
