Management summary

The Information Security Management System (ISMS) Policy, abbreviated BeFresh-ISMS, defines the requirements for the ISMS of BeFresh, in accordance with the requirements listed in ISO/IEC 27001.

It defines:



- the context including stakeholder expectations;
- leadership including management commitment and delegation of roles and responsibilities to staff;
- planning covering methods to identify actions to address risks and actions to implement objectives or even avoid non-compliance.
- support covering the management of resources, supplements, awareness, communication and documents;
- operation, covering the implementation of the actions identified according to the planning;
- performance evaluation covering the monitoring and self-assessment of ISMS document follow-up, internal audit, and the management review carried out by the Managing Director, CISO, CIO, and related to specific topics individual members of the ISMS team and the Security Event Team (SET) are consulted;
- continuous improvement describing the requirements to analyse non-conformities and implement corrective actions and manage suggestions for improvement.

## Introduction

### Context

According to the Information Security policy, BeFresh intends to implement all relevant security safeguards to protect their own information and their customers’ information. BeFresh therefore decided to deploy an Information Security Management System (ISMS) according to the international standard ISO/IEC 27001 [16], as described in the present policy.

### Objectives

This policy defines the requirements for the ISMS of BeFresh. This system is the governance tool of information security for all information processed by BeFresh. It meets the requirements listed in ISO/IEC 27001.

The chapters in this document have been structured and labelled according to the structure of that standard. The document addresses, chapter by chapter, the requirements of the standard and transposes them to requirements applicable to BeFresh. Requirements are sometimes refined or completed then with additional recommendations.

### Scope and audience

The scope of this policy is identical to the one of the Information Security policy of BeFresh.

This document shall be read by people with responsibility for the ISMS, in particular the CISO and top management.

### Understanding the organization and its context

The organization shall determine external and internal issues that are relevant to its purpose and that affect its ability to achieve the intended outcome(s) of its information security management system.

BeFresh encourages and supports its employees to engage in proper management of security. It also supports the reliability of its consulting advice by applying it first internally.

BeFresh manages a multitude of information which shall be protected adequately for the welfare -of its interested parties. That is why the security policy requires following good practices in security management and data protection.

Top management is committed in supporting information security. It has been decided to set up a security policy since 2025, which is achieved with the implementation of this document and the related requirements. It was reviewed to be fully compliant to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 [16] and aligned to the ISMS of the State of Luxembourg.

NOTE Determining these issues refers to establishing the external and internal context of the organization considered in Clause 5.4.1 of ISO 31000:2018.

### Understanding the needs and expectations of interested parties

The organization shall determine:



- interested parties that are relevant to the information security management system;
- the relevant requirements of these interested parties;
- which of these requirements will be addressed through the information security management system.

The expectations of interested parties in information security are varied and depend on the situation of each of them. Their requirements help determine the security objectives.

For customers: customers make confidential information available to BeFresh, the disclosure of which could harm their economic model. BeFresh is responsible for the adequate protection of this information according to the contractual agreement and business practices

For partners: the partners agree on mutual knowledge transfer and assistance  service provisioning and they expect that such information remains confidential with respect to other companies. The nondisclosure agreement (NDA) between BeFresh and partners and general business practices define Intellectual Property Right (IPR) and security requirements of this information.

For employees and candidates: they rely on information related to their Curriculum Vitae, health, economic situation, and willingness to change jobs, to be protected against abuse and negative impact.

For CNPD: ensuring compliance with the GDPR.

For ‘Autorité Nationale de Sécurité (ANS)’: BeFresh is expected to follow laws and regulations on classified data, and to report any breach or incidents related to such data as well as changes related to staff having a security clearance to ANS.

In addition, all information provided by BeFresh, either to employees, enterprises, partners, or public is integer in all circumstances to maintain the trust of these parties in the good management of information security by BeFresh.

Finally, interested parties expect quick reactions from BeFresh, which presupposes that information and information systems are available to perform the expected treatment in due time.

NOTE The requirements can include legal and regulatory requirements and contractual obligations.

## Leadership

Top management shall demonstrate leadership and commitment with respect to the information security management system by:



- ensuring the information security policy and the information security objectives are established and are compatible with the strategic direction of the organization;
- ensuring the integration of the information security management system requirements into the organization’s processes;
- ensuring that the resources needed for the information security management system are available;
- communicating the importance of effective information security management and of conforming to the information security management system requirements;
- ensuring that the information security management system achieves its intended outcome(s);
- directing and supporting persons to contribute to the effectiveness of the information security management system;
- promoting continual improvement; and
- supporting other relevant management roles to demonstrate their leadership as it applies to their areas of responsibility.

BeFresh is strongly committed to taking all economically justifiable steps to ensure the security of staff, data, products, services, premises, and assets.

Top management exercises leadership by establishing information security policies, supporting all involved parties in implementation, and revising them if necessary. These policies include general and specific security objectives, including an ISMS (See this document).

Top management affirms its commitment to the management system and ensured the achievement of the objectives set. Its leadership is defined by the multiple commitments set out in ISMS documents.

## Planning

### Actions to address risks and opportunities

#### General

When planning for the information security management system, the organization shall consider the issues referred to in 4.1 and the requirements referred to in 4.2 and determine the risks and opportunities that need to be addressed to:



- ensure the information security management system can achieve its intended outcome(s);
- prevent, or reduce, undesired effects;
- achieve continual improvement.

The organization shall plan:



- actions to address these risks and opportunities; and
- how to
    - integrate and implement the actions into its information security management system processes; and
    - evaluate the effectiveness of these actions.

Planning consists in establishing an action plan to address the risks and an action plan to ensure that objectives are achieved.

#### Information security risk assessment

The organization shall define and apply an information security risk assessment process that:



- establishes and maintains information security risk criteria that include:
- criteria for performing information security risk assessments;
- ensures that repeated information security risk assessments produce consistent, valid and comparable results;
- identifies the information security risks:
    - apply the information security risk assessment process to identify risks associated with the loss of confidentiality, integrity and availability for information within the scope of the information security management system; and
    - identify the risk owners;
- analyses the information security risks:
    - assess the potential consequences that would result if the risks identified in 6.1.2 c) 1) were to materialize;
    - assess the realistic likelihood of the occurrence of the risks identified in 6.1.2 c) 1); and
    - determine the levels of risk;
- evaluates the information security risks:
    - compare the results of risk analysis with the risk criteria established in 6.1.2 a); and
    - prioritize the analysed risks for risk treatment.

The organization shall retain documented information about the information security risk assessment process.

The risk assessment process consists in:

establishing and maintaining the information security risk criteria including:

risk acceptance criteria, and;

criteria for performing information security risk assessments.

ensuring that repeated information security risk assessments produce consistent, valid, and comparable results;

identifying information security risks:

by considering risks associated with the loss of confidentiality, integrity, and availability for all assets in scope, and;

by assigning each identified risk to a risk owner;

analysing the information security risks:

by assessing potential consequences if the risks materialize;

by assessing the realistic likelihood of the occurrence of identified risks, and;

by determining the risk levels;

evaluating the information security risks:

by comparing the results of risk analysis with the risk criteria established in a, and;

by prioritizing the analysed risks for risk treatment;

approving the documented result of the previous steps by the risk owners and either the BeFresh board or a management review meeting.

The objectives and methodology of risk assessment are provided in BeFresh-RiskMgt #161.

In case of use of a specific process for a given risk or entity, this process should be documented and approved according to the ISMS practices.

#### Information security risk treatment

The organization shall define and apply an information security risk treatment process to:



- select appropriate information security risk treatment options, taking account of the risk assessment results;
- determine all controls that are necessary to implement the information security risk treatment option(s) chosen;
- NOTE 3 The information security controls listed in Annex A are not exhaustive and additional information security controls can be included if needed.
- justification for their inclusion;
- whether the necessary controls are implemented or not; and
- the justification for excluding any of the Annex A controls.
- obtain risk owners’ approval of the information security risk treatment plan and acceptance of the residual information security risks.

The organization shall retain documented information about the information security risk treatment process.

BeFresh applies its own risk treatment process, based on the ISO/IEC 27005 standard. The process allows to:



- select appropriate information security risk treatment options, considering the risk assessment results;

determine all controls necessary to implement the chosen treatment options;

compare those controls with those indicated in the policy documents issued by BeFresh and check that no necessary control has been omitted;

produce a statement of applicability containing all necessary measures, the reasons for their inclusion or exclusion, and the fact that they are already implemented or not;

develop an information security risk treatment plan containing controls not implemented except those for which risk analysis provides the justification that they are not required to reduce the risk to a situation acceptable and accepted by the leader;

obtain the approval of the risk owners of the information security risk treatment plan and their acceptance of the residual information security risks;

get the approval of the Managing Director of the total residual risk and the risk treatment plan. This plan will identify actions at least for the budget period that follows.

The objective and methodology of risk treatment are provided in BeFresh-RiskMgt #161

In case of use of a specific process for a given risk or entity, this process should be documented and approved according to the ISMS practices.

NOTE 4 The information security risk assessment and treatment process in this document aligns with the principles and generic guidelines provided in ISO 31000.

### Information security objectives and planning to achieve them

The organization shall establish information security objectives at relevant functions and levels.

The information security objectives shall:



- be consistent with the information security policy;
- be measurable (if practicable);
- take into account applicable information security requirements, and results from risk assessment and risk treatment;
- be monitored;
- be communicated;
- be updated as appropriate;
- be available as documented information.

The organization shall retain documented information on the information security objectives.

When planning how to achieve its information security objectives, the organization shall determine:



- what will be done;
- what resources will be required;
- who will be responsible;
- when it will be completed; and
- how the results will be evaluated.

The authors of the security objectives ensure that these objectives:

are measurable (whenever possible);

consider the security requirements;

consider the results of the assessment and treatment of risks.

BeFresh establishes, in line with the Information Security policy, security objectives for the various security management domains after studying its own context and its specific risk situation and formulating if needed objectives that complement the overall objectives of ISO/IEC 27002. It communicates them internally, e.g. via the ISMS repository, and updates them when necessary.

CISO establishes an exhaustive list of actions needed to achieve the objectives. Based on this list, BeFresh establishes a plan, which determines for every action the work to be performed, the required resources, the person accountable, the deadlines, and the process of evaluating the effectiveness of the action.

This plan called Security Action Plan (SAP) is a tactical document, since it reflects the will to implement the objectives of the Information Security policy in a given period of time.

This plan is updated:

by adding a compliance check every time a new document or a new version of a document comes into force,

by adding actions because of a compliance check, an audit, or an incident handling;

by adding actions because of effectiveness evaluation of actions;

by removing action that have been implemented and checked.

This plan consists in a set of ISMS tickets managed in the Jira ticketing tool. The coordination of this plan is a key activity of the ISMS team.

NOTE Applicable actions can include, for example: the provision of training to, the mentoring of, or the re-assignment of current employees; or the hiring or contracting of competent persons.

### Awareness

Persons doing work under the organization’s control shall be aware of:



- the information security policy;
- their contribution to the effectiveness of the information security management system, including the benefits of improved information security performance; and
- the implications of not conforming with the information security management system requirements.

To manage awareness of information security, BeFresh has set up an awareness program and related indicators to measure awareness.

The staff members of BeFresh participate in an initial information security and data protection training including a presentation of the company’s information security policy.

Staff members are informed of the need to contribute to the proper functioning of the ISMS and to its improvement by submitting suggestions for improvement. They also receive regular reminders at a frequency that depends on performance indicators to be established for measuring awareness.

In addition, staff members regularly participate in security awareness campaigns defined in an awareness and training program established for BeFresh. BeFresh ensures that an adequate number of staff members have followed such awareness training within six months after their employment.

### Documented information

#### General

The organization’s information security management system shall include:



- documented information required by this document; and
- documented information determined by the organization as being necessary for the effectiveness of the information security management system.

The ISMS is based on a series of documents that define obligations for all staff and third parties or subcontractors. The ISMS documents are classified into five levels:

Security policies (POL): documents reflecting the intentions and direction of BeFresh as formally expressed by its top management. They consist of the Information Security policy which contains the strategic goals and key principles of information security within the context of the organization, and high-level documents which contain tactical indications on how strategic goals will be achieved in a given sector or domain, such as the ISMS policy which defines the ISMS of BeFresh based on the requirements of ISO/IEC 27001[16], the security organization policy, the risk management policy, or the asset management policy.

Plans (PLA): documents containing tactical indications of when and how objectives will be achieved in a given sector or domain, such as risk treatment plan, action plan to achieve objectives, or business continuity plan.

Procedures (PRO): documents describing the processes and the responsibilities of actors, such as the document management procedure or a computer installation procedure.

Standards (STA): contain instructions, guidelines, management follow-up lists, security rules related to classified information, such as the list of allowed cryptographic tools, reports of risk assessments, glossary, etc.

Records (REC): any other element or anything that can serve as evidence of the correct execution of information security management, such as system logs or meeting minutes.

All documents required by the Information Security policy are assigned to one of these levels and have a unique identification.

NOTE The extent of documented information for an information security management system can differ from one organization to another due to:

the size of organization and its type of activities, processes, products and services;

the complexity of processes and their interactions; and

the competence of persons.

#### Creating and updating

When creating and updating documented information the organization shall ensure appropriate:



- identification and description (e.g. a title, date, author, or reference number);
- format (e.g. language, software version, graphics) and media (e.g. paper, electronic); and
- review and approval for suitability and adequacy.

Documents of type policy, procedure, or standard, carry an identification and a description including:

the type of document or the name of the customer;

the project or domain

the title of the document;

the modification, publication, and validation dates;

the author of the document or the author of its modifications;

the sequence and version numbers.

Documents are written in English language.

Documents are approved by the appropriate staff after analysing the adequacy and relevance of the document content.

This information security policy is regularly reviewed and updated if needed. The CISO or a delegated author is responsible for changing the document. A new version is officially approved by the author (generally the CISO), a quality reviewer (the CISO if he is not the author), and the document owner (generally the Managing Director) and then communicated by the CISO to the targeted audience.

If major changes are prepared, the ISMS team and the document owner should be consulted before implementation.

#### Control of documented information

Documented information required by the information security management system and by this document shall be controlled to ensure:



- it is available and suitable for use, where and when it is needed; and
- it is adequately protected (e.g. from loss of confidentiality, improper use, or loss of integrity).

For the control of documented information, the organization shall address the following activities, as applicable:



- distribution, access, retrieval and use;
- storage and preservation, including the preservation of legibility;
- control of changes (e.g. version control); and
- retention and disposition.

Documented information of external origin, determined by the organization to be necessary for the planning and operation of the information security management system, shall be identified as appropriate, and controlled.

The ISMS documents are stored in a suitable place and are properly protected depending on their degree of confidentiality and integrity.

The CISO is in charge of their distribution, storage, preservation, change control, and deletion at the end of the agreed lifetime.

Documented information of external origin, determined by the CISO to be necessary for the planning and operation of the information security management system (e.g., IT charter of a service provider, the management rules of the network of BeFresh, reference documents) are also identified and controlled by the internal document management rules (cf. BeFresh-ListDoc, #0D).

The current versions of validated, publicly classified ISMS documents are available to all staff members on the BeFresh shared drive \InfoSec. Restricted documents should be available only to the ISMS team, the SET team, the internal auditor, and board members.

NOTE Access can imply a decision regarding the permission to view the documented information only, or the permission and authority to view and change the documented information, etc.

## Operation

### Operational planning and control

The organization shall plan, implement and control the processes needed to meet requirements, and to implement the actions determined in Clause 6, by:



- establishing criteria for the processes;
- implementing control of the processes in accordance with the criteria.

Documented information shall be available to the extent necessary to have confidence that the processes have been carried out as planned.

The organization shall control planned changes and review the consequences of unintended changes, taking action to mitigate any adverse effects, as necessary.

The organization shall ensure that externally provided processes, products or services that are relevant to the information security management system are controlled.

The Managing Director delegates to CISO the tasks of:

planning, implementing and controlling the processes necessary to satisfy the requirements defined in the policies, plans, procedures, and standards of the information security policy;

monitoring the implementation of actions of the SAP [ISMS6.2] and of the risk treatment plan;

maintaining these plans according to the progress of work and changing priorities;

documenting the rationale for changes to these plans;

defining the way to check or to declare that an action has been completed successfully.

The CISO may delegate some tasks to a CISO assistant or to a System Security Manager.

### Information security risk assessment

The organization shall perform information security risk assessments at planned intervals or when significant changes are proposed or occur, taking account of the criteria established in 6.1.2 a).

The organization shall retain documented information of the results of the information security risk assessments.

BeFresh conducts information security risk assessments at planned intervals according to a multi-annual plan and when significant changes are proposed or occur.

Information risks include in the context all risks relating to the protection of personal data.

BeFresh stores the documented information on the results of the assessment of information security risks process.

The risk assessment is based on the process and criteria established by BeFresh in its risk management policy #161, supplemented if needed by specific guidance. A report containing the results of these assessments is updated at least once a year.

### Information security risk treatment

The organization shall implement the information security risk treatment plan.

The organization shall retain documented information of the results of the information security risk treatment.

As for the risk assessment, BeFresh performs information security risk treatment and updates it at planned intervals according to a multi-year plan.

BeFresh keeps the documented information on the results of the information security risk treatment process.

Risk treatment follows the process and criteria established by BeFresh in its risk management policy #161 supplemented if needed by specific guidance.

An overview showing the implementation progress of all actions in the risk treatment plan and the actions generated by security incidents handling is updated regularly, at least every quarter.

The security controls that BeFresh intends to implement are chosen using a risk assessment framework based on a documented methodology #175. This method should allow risks to be minimized and the costs involving security to remain economic. To manage risk, all risks have to be correctly understood, analyzed, documented, and accepted.

### Management review

#### General

Top management shall review the organization's information security management system at planned intervals to ensure its continuing suitability, adequacy and effectiveness.

A management review is performed during a meeting whose aim is to conduct a formal evaluation of the ISMS by top management and to ensure its continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness. The Managing Director chairs it.

The management review is the monitoring step that assesses the past in order to redefine an action plan, to release resources for the next cycle, and to reaffirm the leadership and commitment to the ISMS.

This step entails top management making decisions to improve information security and to enforce proper control on information and communication.

The management review is carried out at least annually. If necessary, an extraordinary meeting is initiated, e.g. in case of major changes, to ensure that the ISMS is progressing in the desired direction and that it remains consistent.

The report of the management review meeting is formally approved by the Managing Director who chairs the meeting.

At the management review meeting, the presence of the following staff is mandatory:

the Managing Director;

the CIO;

the CISO;

the CISO assistant.

Or their respective delegates. Board members and other ISMS team members should be invited too. Top management or the CISO can invite other key staff members or security experts.

In general, the CISO is responsible for the preparation and documentation of the management review.

#### Management review inputs

The management review shall include consideration of:



- the status of actions from previous management reviews;
- changes in external and internal issues that are relevant to the information security management system;
- changes in needs and expectations of interested parties that are relevant to the information security management system;
- feedback on the information security performance, including trends in:
    - nonconformities and corrective actions;
    - monitoring and measurement results;
    - audit results;
    - fulfilment of information security objectives;
- feedback from interested parties;
- results of risk assessment and status of risk treatment plan;
- opportunities for continual improvement.

To decide objectively, participants in the management review need various information. Some of the required documents are prepared by the CISO.

These documents are:

documentation of the ISMS, the list of changes, and the Statement of Applicability (SoA);

the reports of independent reviews (internal audit and external audit, if applicable);

the report of the risk assessment and treatment, the assessment of threats, vulnerabilities, preventive and corrective actions;

the report of event management and security incidents;

the status of plans (audit program, awareness spreading program, security action plan, risk treatment plan, list of exemptions, follow-up of recommendations, list of actions decided at the previous management review);

monitoring and measurement results;

the status of compliance with the documents of the security policy;

the feedback and suggestions for improvement from the various parties.

#### Management review results

The results of the management review shall include decisions related to continual improvement opportunities and any needs for changes to the information security management system.

Documented information shall be available as evidence of the results of management reviews.

The following actions are to be systematically covered or reviewed:

## Improvement

### Continual improvement

The organization shall continually improve the suitability, adequacy and effectiveness of the information security management system.

The information treatment processes, and any documents related to the ISMS are reviewed annually or in case of significant changes, e.g. change of the scope of the ISMS, change in governmental strategy or policy, technological change, change of risks, change of the legal framework. The standard BeFresh-ListDoc #0D indicates the status of each document and the date of the next revision.

Each document owner is responsible for reviewing and change, if necessary, his documents.

The reviews of policies consider the results of management reviews.

BeFresh makes available to its employees a system to submit suggestions for improvement. Similarly, interested stakeholders can submit suggestions by email to info@befresh.lu or by any communication to their contact persons at BeFresh, who will then encode the suggestion in a Jira ticket.

The CISO is responsible for dealing with suggestions and if useful presenting them during the next ISMS team meeting for discussion.

All participants to meetings are allowed and encouraged to add suggestions for improvement at the end of the meeting. This applies to ISMS meetings, SET meetings, BeFresh meetings, management review meetings, and board meetings.

### Nonconformity and corrective action

When a nonconformity occurs, the organization shall:



- react to the nonconformity, and as applicable:
- deal with the consequences;
- evaluate the need for action to eliminate the causes of nonconformity, in order that it does not recur or occur elsewhere, by:
    - reviewing the nonconformity;
    - determining the causes of the nonconformity; and
    - determining if similar nonconformities exist, or could potentially occur;
- implement any action needed;
- review the effectiveness of any corrective action taken; and
- make changes to the information security management system, if necessary.

Corrective actions shall be appropriate to the effects of the nonconformities encountered.

Documented information shall be available as evidence of:



- the nature of the nonconformities and any subsequent actions taken,
- the results of any corrective action.

Nonconformity is identified through the following:



- compliance checks;

ad hoc review by the CISO;

due care by any person reporting events to the SET;

internal/external audits.

Upon identification of nonconformity, appropriate corrective actions are established by the CISO and tracked. The status of all nonconformity and corrective actions are evaluated during the management review.

Nonconformity identified by employees of BeFresh shall be immediately reported to the SET, except if they are documented on the same day in a compliance check report or an audit report.

For each nonconformity, BeFresh uses Jira tickets or audit reports to document:



- the response for controlling, correcting, and dealing with the consequences;

an assessment of whether it is necessary to take action to eliminate the causes and discover similar nonconformity;

an implementation of actions required to remedy the effects of non-compliance (corrective actions).

The SET meeting ensures:



- a review of the effectiveness of every corrective action;

a change of the ISMS, if required, to improve it;

adequate documentation of all these elements and decisions.

If a non-conformity is found, the CISO or another SET member executes the following process, i.e.:



- determines the causes of the non-compliance ;

assesses the need for action to establish compliance

Implements the appropriate corrective action;

reviews the corrective action taken to verify its effectiveness and identify any deficiencies or weaknesses

documents the analyses and actions in a review report (e.g. an email).

Several types of Jira tickets exist to react on non-conformities:



- Action/Corrective

Action/External Audit

Action/Internal Audit

Security Event

Security incident/Minor

Security incident/Major.

Once the corrective action has been implemented, the action is declared as “Feedback” and assigned either to a person in charge of the source or to the CISO (or his delegates).

Once this person has checked the effectiveness of the action, he declares the ticket as resolved. If this is not the case, he declares it as “Problem” and return it for resolution to an adequate person.

This person should indicate in the ticket how he checked this effectiveness.
